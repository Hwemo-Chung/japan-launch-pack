PicBatch: a Japanese store listing, from competitor research to final drafts

This is my own app, not a client. It is pre-launch, so there are no ranking results to show. This page shows the process and deliverables.

PicBatch is an Android app (Kotlin, package com.picbatch.app) for on-device batch photo processing: select many photos, resize them, convert their format and choose where to save the results. It collects no data. It is currently in closed testing and not yet on Google Play.

The drafts below were written by a Tokyo-based engineer using AI-assisted research, then reviewed by hand. I'm not a native Japanese speaker. Each Japanese draft has an English gloss next to it so you can see what it says.

How the research was done

  1. Picked three searches a Japanese user would type to find an app like this: 画像 一括 リサイズ (batch image resize), 写真 圧縮 (photo compression) and 画像 変換 (image conversion).
  2. Ran each search on Google Play Japan (lang=ja, country=jp, up to 15 results) with scripts/jp_competitors.py play, and ran the first search once on the Japan App Store with scripts/jp_competitors.py ios for comparison.
  3. Extracted the most frequent keywords across the competitors' titles and descriptions, counted by how many apps use each one.
  4. Chose a keyword set, wrote the drafts and checked every length-limited field with scripts/jp_competitors.py check.

Data collected on 2026-10-17. Search results change over time. The raw JSON output is in case-study/data/ in this site's repository.

Competitors on Google Play Japan

The 10 highest-placed unique apps across the two searches closest to what PicBatch does. The rank columns show where each app appeared in each search (– means it wasn't in that search's results).

What stands out

  • Almost nobody says "batch" in the title. Only 1 of the 11 titles for 画像 一括 リサイズ contains 一括 (batch), and none of the 15 for 写真 圧縮 do. On the Japan App Store, 7 of 14 titles for the same search include it. Batch processing is PicBatch's main feature, so it goes in the title.
  • English-only titles still show up. 2 of 11 results for the resize search and 4 of 15 for the compression search have titles with no Japanese at all. A clear Japanese title is a reasonable way to stand out among them.
  • 画像 変換 means something else. In that search, 5 of 15 titles mention PDF and the top results are mostly PDF and JPG converters. Users typing it often want "photo to PDF", which PicBatch doesn't do, so the drafts say 形式変換 (format conversion) rather than chasing the broad term.
  • Titles use most of the limit. Average title length is 22 characters for the resize search. Several competitors are at exactly 30, and one title (…サイズ変更、共) appears to be cut off mid-word, which looks careless in search results.

Chosen keyword set

Each keyword has to match both what people search for and what the app really does. Words for features PicBatch doesn't have were left out, even when competitors use them a lot.

KeywordMeaningSearch intentWhy it's in
一括 / まとめて / 複数batch / all together / multiple"I have a lot of photos and don't want to do them one by one."The core feature. 一括 is rare in Play titles (1 of 11 and 0 of 15), so it differentiates. まとめて and 複数 are the everyday ways to say it.
写真 / 画像photo / imageBoth words are used for the same thing.The top words in every search. Users type either one, so the title carries both.
リサイズ / サイズ変更resize / change size"Make this picture smaller in pixels."リサイズ is in 13 of 14 iOS titles and 5 of 11 Play titles. サイズ変更 is the plain Japanese phrasing for users who don't use the loanword.
縮小 / 容量shrink / file size, storage"The file is too big to send or store."Used by 8–10 apps in the resize and compression searches. Resizing reduces file size, so these describe a real result of using the app.
圧縮compressSame goal as above: smaller files.Very common (12 of 15 apps for 写真 圧縮). Used in the iOS keyword field and in the description only in the sense of "making files smaller by resizing", not as a claim about compression settings.
形式変換format conversion"Change this image to another file type."A real feature. Phrased as 形式変換 instead of the broad 画像 変換, whose results are dominated by PDF converters.
保存先save location"Where do the processed files go?"A real feature that many competitors don't mention in their titles. 保存 appears in 8–11 apps per search.
端末内で処理 / データ収集なしprocessed on the device / no data collectedNot a search term. It answers "is it safe to put my photos in this?"Used in the short description and a screenshot caption to help a user decide after they find the app.

Left out on purpose

  • pdf: high frequency in the conversion search, but PicBatch doesn't make PDFs.
  • png, jpeg, webp, heic: very common, but format names should only appear for formats the app supports. They go into the full description once the supported list is final.
  • トリミング (crop), AI, 編集 (edit): not features of this app.
  • 無料 (free), 簡単 (easy): Google Play's metadata policy doesn't allow price or promotional wording in the title, and "easy" says nothing specific.

Full description (opening lines)

PicBatchは、たくさんの写真や画像をまとめて処理するアプリです。 複数の写真を選んで、リサイズ（サイズ変更）や形式変換を一括で行えます。保存先も自分で選べます。 処理はすべて端末内で行います。写真をサーバーにアップロードすることはなく、データも収集しません。 【主な機能】 ・複数の写真・画像を選んで一括リサイズ ・画像の形式をまとめて変換 ・保存先フォルダを選択

PicBatch is an app for processing lots of photos and images at once.

Select multiple photos and resize them (change their size) or convert their format in one go. You can also choose where to save them.

All processing happens on your device. Photos are never uploaded to a server, and no data is collected.

Main features: batch-resize multiple photos and images; convert image formats in bulk; choose the save folder.

186 characters (counted with Python len(); the Google Play limit for the full description is 4,000). The first lines matter most because they show before "more". The full version adds the supported formats and step-by-step usage.
