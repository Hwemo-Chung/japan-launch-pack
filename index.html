Why Japan

Japan is a large app market, and many indie developers never try to reach it. When they do, they usually ship an English-only listing or a quick translation.

Japanese store pages work differently. Users often expect a lot of information on the page, and screenshots commonly carry descriptive captions rather than short slogans. Keyword search also works differently, because Japanese is written without spaces between words. A direct translation of your English metadata misses most of this.

The gap this fills

Today indie developers mostly have two options:

  • Cheap translation gigs. You get your English text translated, and that's it. Nobody does keyword research, looks at competitors or adapts the copy to each store's limits.
  • Localization and ASO agencies. They do the full job, but they typically charge $500 or more per project or ask for a monthly retainer.

Japan Launch Pack is in between. It covers the research and store-specific work at a fixed price that makes sense for an indie app.

FAQ

Is this just translation?

No. Translation is one part of the job. The package also includes keyword research for Japanese search, analysis of the top Japanese competitors in your category, copy written to fit each store's character limits, and screenshot captions written for how Japanese store pages are read. The PDF report explains the reasoning behind the choices.

Are you a native Japanese speaker?

No. I'm an app engineer who lives and works in Tokyo. The Japanese copy is written by me with AI-assisted research, and a human reviews all of it before delivery. An optional native proofread may be offered later, but it isn't included now and isn't promised. If your app needs native-level copy from day one, say so in your application and I'll give you an honest answer about whether this package is the right fit.

What if my app is not a fit?

I'll reply by email and tell you so, with a short explanation where I can. You won't be charged anything, because no payment is collected when you apply.

Do you guarantee rankings or downloads?

No. Nobody can honestly guarantee App Store or Google Play rankings. What you get is Japanese metadata and keywords based on research into your category, built to give your listing a better starting point in Japan.

What do I need to provide?

Your app's store URL(s) and a short description of what the app does and who it's for. If your app isn't live yet, describe it in the notes and I'll tell you what else I need. Once the scope is confirmed, I may ask a few follow-up questions by email.

Can you localize in-app strings?

Not as part of this package for now. If you're interested, mention it in your application notes and I'll let you know if and when it becomes available.
