Japanese keyword field tool

Paste keywords separated by commas, Japanese commas, spaces or new lines. The tool removes duplicates, joins them with commas and no spaces, and counts characters against the App Store and Google Play limits. Nothing you type leaves your browser.

iOS keyword field (100 characters)

"Fit to 100" drops keywords from the end of your list until the result fits, so put the most important ones first.

Other fields

Characters are counted as Unicode code points, so one kanji, kana or emoji counts as one. The stores' own counters are the final word.

Why Japanese keyword fields are different

  • No spaces between words. Japanese is written without spaces, so you choose where one keyword ends and the next begins. Separate them with commas only; a space after a comma wastes a character.
  • Don't repeat title words. On the App Store, words in the app name and subtitle are already indexed, so repeating them in the keyword field uses up space for nothing.
  • Translation is not keyword research. A literal translation of your English keywords often misses the words Japanese users actually type. Look at what the top local apps in your category use.
